ClawTalk support

Contact jkieley@strboard.com for app support or privacy requests. Include your app version, iOS version and a description of the problem. Do not email API keys, private pairing codes, passwords or sensitive conversation content.

Getting connected

ClawTalk needs a compatible OpenClaw companion server. Ask your server operator for a fresh pairing QR code or pairing JSON. Open Settings and scan or import it. Keep pairing material private. A server invitation is separate from an Apple account.

Microphone and voice

Allow microphone access in iPhone Settings to record. Hold the talk control, speak, then release to send. Voice generation and transcription require the server's provider access. Text replies may be available even if voice fails. Check volume and the app's voice/playback settings.

Replies and notifications

Check connection status and return to the conversation to recover retained replies. For uncertain delivery, use the app's recovery controls rather than sending duplicate requests. Allow notifications in iPhone Settings; your server must also be configured for Apple push notifications.

Privacy controls

Settings → Privacy & diagnostics contains Automatic diagnostics and Verbose UI snapshots switches. Turning them off clears their corresponding unsent diagnostics. Removing a conversation or instance locally does not delete copies on your server or at AI providers. Contact your server operator for server-side deletion and retention questions.

Self-hosting

The companion server and its provider credentials are administered separately from the iPhone app. The app does not contain provider API keys. Ask your server operator about provider billing, supported models, access invitations and data handling.
