ClawTalk Personal

A native iPhone companion for your own OpenClaw assistant. Type or hold to talk, read and replay replies, and keep separate conversations and server connections.

ClawTalk requires a compatible, separately configured OpenClaw companion server and pairing invitation. Model, transcription, voice and notification availability depend on that server. ClawTalk does not include a hosted AI subscription.

Built around conversations

  • Send text, recorded audio and selected attachments.
  • Read replies, listen to generated speech and revisit saved history.
  • Switch conversations and paired instances.
  • Choose supported model and voice options, and manage diagnostics in Settings.

Requires iOS 26 or later and an internet connection for server requests. AI responses can be inaccurate; review important information and actions.
