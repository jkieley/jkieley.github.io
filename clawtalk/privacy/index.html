ClawTalk privacy policy

Effective October 28, 2026. ClawTalk Personal is developed by James Kieley. Contact jkieley@strboard.com about this policy or privacy requests.

Your server determines where requests go

ClawTalk is an iPhone client for a separately operated OpenClaw companion server. Pairing connects the app to that server. Messages are not processed entirely on the phone. Your server operator controls its AI providers, tools, configuration and retention. Only connect to a server you trust. Developer-operated review infrastructure uses OpenAI for assistant responses and ElevenLabs for speech transcription and generated voice.

Conversation data and providers

When you send a request, the app transmits your text, recorded audio, selected photos, extracted document text and relevant conversation information to the paired server over an authenticated encrypted connection. The server may send this content and conversation context to its configured AI and speech providers to fulfill the request. Tools enabled by the operator can process or share information with additional services when carrying out a request. Provider terms and retention apply separately: OpenAI privacy policy and ElevenLabs privacy policy. Do not send information you do not want the server operator or configured providers to process.

Device identity and notifications

The app stores a device signing identity in the iPhone Keychain/Secure Enclave and registers its public key with the server. The server uses device and request identifiers to authenticate access and deliver the right replies. Provider API credentials remain on the server. If notifications are enabled, an Apple push token is registered with the server. Notification payloads may include conversation titles and response previews and are sent through Apple's push service. You control notification permissions and previews in iPhone Settings.

Diagnostics and screen snapshots

Automatic diagnostics and Verbose UI snapshots are currently enabled by default for paired owners. In Settings → Privacy & diagnostics, you can turn either off. Automatic diagnostics includes timing, connectivity, app/device versions, error and activity information. Verbose snapshots additionally transmit the app screenshot, visible text, interface hierarchy, navigation and layout state, and selected-instance conversation, draft, queue and activity state. These snapshots can contain personal messages and unsent drafts. Sensitive setup/access screens omit screenshots and redact interface text; stored credentials are excluded. Diagnostics go to your paired server and can be made available to its assistant for troubleshooting. A submitted issue report may also include the screenshot and diagnostics shown in its report flow.

Storage and retention

The app keeps local settings, paired-instance information, saved messages, recordings/replies and pending work to support offline access and recovery. The companion bridge normally retains jobs and audio for 48 hours; history recovery returns up to the latest 30 retained replies. OpenClaw conversation records and provider retention are separate and may last longer. Automatic telemetry is bounded to seven days on the server. Verbose snapshots are bounded to 12 snapshots/32 MB/three days while unsent on the phone and 200 snapshots/256 MB/seven days on the companion server; size limits may remove older records sooner. Operator backups, support artifacts and separately retained records may follow different retention periods.

Your choices and deletion

You choose what to send and which server to pair. Microphone and camera access require iOS permission. Disable diagnostics in the app to stop the corresponding collection and clear its unsent queue. Deleting local conversations or removing an instance does not erase server, provider or backup copies. Contact your server operator to request access to or deletion of those records. For developer-operated review/support data, use the contact above and identify the relevant instance or request without sending private credentials.

Advertising and tracking

The app does not include advertising or cross-app advertising tracking SDKs. Data is used to provide the requested assistant features, authenticate the connection, deliver notifications and diagnose app behavior, not to serve targeted ads.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app or its data handling changes. The effective date above identifies this version.
